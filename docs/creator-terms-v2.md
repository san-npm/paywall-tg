# Gategram Creator Terms (v2)

Effective and last updated: 2026-08-17
Operator: **COMMIT MEDIA S.A R.L.** (VAT LU34811132, RCS B276192)

These Creator Terms form part of Gategram's Terms of Service and govern creators who publish paid offers through Gategram.

## Commercial terms

- Buyers pay the Gategram bot using Telegram Stars. Creators do not receive Stars directly from Telegram buyers.
- After a successful payment, Gategram records a creator share after a platform fee of up to 5%. Because fees must be whole Stars, the fee is rounded down and never exceeds 5%.
- Refunds and reversals reduce the corresponding creator payable amount.
- Creator Stars are converted to EUR using Gategram's published payout rate. The rate reflects the Telegram bot-developer reward and Gategram's foreign-exchange, liquidity, network, and operating reserves.

## Holding period, threshold, and invoices

- Earnings remain on hold for at least 21 days after purchase.
- A creator may request a payout after at least 1,000 cleared Stars are available.
- The EUR rate and gross EUR amount are locked when Gategram creates a payout record.
- Gategram issues a payout statement. The creator submits a valid invoice to COMMIT MEDIA S.A R.L. for the payable amount unless a separate written self-billing agreement applies.
- Published payout rates and thresholds may change for future payouts if Telegram's reward, withdrawal, foreign-exchange, or operating conditions change. Existing payout records retain their locked rate.

## Refunds, risk, and support

- Gategram may delay, reserve, or reverse amounts affected by refunds, fraud, sanctions, disputes, platform holds, or legal requirements.
- Creators must accurately describe their products and provide content that Gategram can deliver after payment.
- Payment and delivery support is available through `/paysupport` or `bob@openletz.com`.

## Content, intellectual property, and tax

- Creators must own or control the rights needed to sell and deliver their content.
- Creators must comply with applicable law, Telegram's terms, and Gategram's policies.
- Creators are responsible for their own registrations, tax declarations, and obligations. Gategram may request information required for compliance.

Mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected.
